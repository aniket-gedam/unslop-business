# Business website patterns

Use these as options, not mandatory templates.

## Hospitality: cafes, restaurants, bakeries, hotels
Priorities:
- Atmosphere and sense of place.
- Menu or offering discovery.
- Hours, address, directions, reservations, ordering.
- Photography that represents the real experience.
- Events, catering, gifting, or multiple locations when relevant.

Avoid:
- Hiding the menu behind vague storytelling.
- Invented opening hours, menus, reviews, or location details.
- Generic food stock imagery that misrepresents the venue.

## Retail and fashion
Priorities:
- Clear category and collection navigation.
- Campaign/editorial imagery.
- Scannable product cards.
- Product detail, sizing/variants, price, availability, delivery/returns.
- Search, filtering, and sorting where catalog size requires them.

Avoid:
- Making every product look identical in scale/crop.
- Hiding price or product identity.
- Fake cart/checkout functionality.

## Premium services and professional businesses
Priorities:
- Clear positioning and outcomes.
- Services organized by customer problem or need.
- Credible proof, case studies, qualifications, or process.
- Clear enquiry path and response expectations.
- Human contact details and service area.

Avoid:
- Empty claims such as "world-class" without evidence.
- Fake client logos, metrics, testimonials, or awards.
- Overly abstract copy that never explains what the business does.

## Local businesses
Priorities:
- Location/service area.
- Contact methods.
- Opening hours or availability.
- Directions, booking, or enquiry.
- Clear service descriptions and trust information.

Avoid:
- Invented addresses, map links, business hours, or credentials.
