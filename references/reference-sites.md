# Initial reference set

These five websites were supplied as the initial inspiration set for `unslop-business`.

The notes below are based on accessible page content and navigation observed during initial research. They are not a claim of pixel-level visual inspection, source-code analysis, exact font identification, or animation measurement. A coding agent should revisit the live URLs with its own browser/screenshot tools before using them for a particular build.

## 1. Roastery Coffee House
URL: https://roasterycoffee.co.in/

Observed content and useful patterns:
- Navigation combines home, shop, café locations, membership, about, menu, journal, and brew guide.
- The homepage positions the brand around modern Indian specialty coffee and café hospitality.
- It connects physical café experience with nationwide coffee retail.
- Brand differentiators and café locations are important content pillars.
- Press/editorial mentions provide credibility and a broader brand story.

Potential lessons:
- Connect product commerce and physical experience without confusing the visitor.
- Use a clear brand proposition followed by differentiators and location discovery.
- Treat editorial content as part of the brand rather than an afterthought.

## 2. Boojee Cafe
URL: https://boojeecafe.com/

Observed content and useful patterns:
- Navigation includes shopping, roastery, locations, contact, careers, and subscriptions.
- The page exposes commerce utilities such as region/currency, search, account, and cart.
- Homepage content includes image-led category discovery for coffee and matcha, bakery, and merchandise.
- Café location discovery and product storytelling are distinct homepage areas.
- Email subscription and service/policy links appear in the footer.

Potential lessons:
- Make product categories visually discoverable.
- Give commerce utilities clear hierarchy without overwhelming the brand.
- Use a useful bridge between online product discovery and physical locations.

## 3. Blackberrys
URL: https://blackberrys.com/

Observed content and useful patterns:
- Navigation has broad product taxonomy, collections, seasonal/wedding stores, login, and cart.
- Category navigation exposes subcategories rather than forcing users to search.
- Homepage merchandising includes campaigns and product listings with names and prices.
- Collection and product discovery are core journeys.

Potential lessons:
- Large catalogs need intentional information architecture.
- Group navigation by customer intent and product taxonomy.
- Product grids should make imagery, product name, price, variants, and availability easy to scan.
- Use campaign placements without making the navigation unusable.

## 4. Cravin' by Andy
URL: https://www.cravinbyandy.com/

Observed content and useful patterns:
- Navigation includes home, about, menu, catering, and gifting.
- Homepage introduces the café and its hospitality positioning.
- Coffee and matcha each receive dedicated editorial/product storytelling.
- Location cards include addresses, hours, and map actions.
- The business presents multiple formats/locations and distinct customer experiences.

Potential lessons:
- Tell the business story through specific products and experiences.
- Make menu, catering, gifting, and location journeys easy to find.
- Location details should be actionable and accurate.
- Keep hospitality copy warm, specific, and grounded.

## 5. Beta Coffee
URL: https://betacoffee.com.au/

Observed content and useful patterns:
- Navigation is concise: shop, café, consulting.
- The homepage uses a distinct point of view and concise brand copy.
- A few operational/business facts are surfaced prominently.
- Café visit information includes address and hours.
- Consulting services are explained through practical capabilities and outcomes.

Potential lessons:
- A small business can have a strong, distinctive point of view.
- Use concise copy and clear information hierarchy.
- Translate complex services into concrete outcomes and examples.
- Do not force a conventional long list of generic marketing sections.

## Shared design lessons to test—not blindly combine

Across the set, useful themes include:
- A strong business identity rather than generic startup styling.
- Editorial storytelling paired with useful commercial navigation.
- Real product, food, fashion, or place imagery.
- Clear discovery paths for products, services, menus, and locations.
- A balance between brand atmosphere and practical information.
- Distinctive typography and composition.
- Conversion paths that match the business model.

The final design must still be tailored to the user's business. A fashion retailer, café, salon, hotel, and professional service firm should not receive the same page architecture.
